## About

Bally Battle is a fast, casual platform fighter. Pick your powers, move around the arena, and knock the other players out.

Host or join a room over the local network. Nearby LAN rooms appear in the lobby, and you can also join with an IP address and port.

## Features

- Three powers: Dash, Spiky, and Bomb
- Multiplayer rooms with nearby LAN discovery
- Desktop and mobile controls
- Pause menu and an in-game tutorial

## Controls

### Desktop

- Move: A / D or Left / Right Arrow
- Jump: W, Space, or Up Arrow
- Dash: Shift or J
- Spiky: E, F, or K
- Bomb: Hold Q to cook, then release to throw
- Power slots: Left, middle, and right mouse buttons use slots 1, 2, and 3

### Mobile

Use the on-screen joystick to move and aim a held bomb. Use the on-screen buttons to jump and activate your equipped powers.
