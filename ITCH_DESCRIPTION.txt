BALLY BATTLE

A fast, casual platform fighter about outplaying your friends with powers.

Pick a power, jump into the arena, and knock the other players out. Dash around, get Spiky, or toss a bomb and send everyone flying. Play together online over your local network, find nearby rooms in the lobby, or join with an IP address and port.

FEATURES

- Three powers to try: Dash, Spiky, and Bomb
- Multiplayer with local network room discovery
- Desktop and mobile controls
- An in-game tutorial that explains the powers
- A pause menu with settings

CONTROLS

Desktop
Move: A / D or Left / Right Arrow
Jump: W, Space, or Up Arrow
Dash: Shift or J
Spiky: E, F, or K
Bomb: Hold Q to cook, then release to throw
Power slots: Left, middle, and right mouse buttons use slots 1, 2, and 3

Mobile
Use the on-screen joystick to move and aim a held bomb. Use the on-screen buttons to jump and activate your equipped powers.
